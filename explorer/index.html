How it works

One clause, followed from words to proof.

Scroll. Each step is computed live in your browser by the Universal Paper engine.

  1. 01

    Write a clause from a shared Term

    The Admin-Fee Term is defined once, in a public library. An agreement only supplies its values: who pays, who is paid, the rate, the period. The sentence is generated from those values, so the words and the data cannot drift apart.

  2. 02

    The agreement gets a fingerprint

    The data is written in one canonical form and hashed. That 64-character fingerprint names this exact agreement. Anyone can recompute it from the agreement alone.

  3. 03

    Change one value and it changes

    The fee moves from 2% to 2.5%. The fingerprint changes almost everywhere; the highlighted characters are the ones that moved. There is no small edit that keeps the same fingerprint.

  4. 04

    Every party signs the fingerprint

    The aggregator and the supplier each sign it. Their contract system, ERP, and spreadsheet each check the signatures and compute the same state, without trusting one another.

  5. 05

    An edited copy is caught by everyone

    Someone changes the rate in the supplier’s copy after signing. The signatures no longer cover it, so that copy is rejected, and the other systems keep the agreement as signed.

Vision · what could be built on it

The paper underneath the apps.

Universal Paper is meant to sit underneath, the way a file format or a network protocol does. Apps for contracting, procurement, pricing, and settlement compete above it. Agents act for parties within signed limits. They work together because they all compute the same fingerprints.

Live events

An illustration of a possible future. The apps are hypothetical and none of them exist yet. The parties, events, signatures, verdicts, and fingerprints are real: each scenario is generated and checked by the Universal Paper engine as you watch.

Apps compete above

A contract system, an ERP, and a procurement tool can each show the same agreement their own way. None of them owns it, and none has to trust another’s copy.

Agents act in a sub-tier

An agent signs only under a Mandate its principal signed, with limits on what it may agree to. An action outside those limits is invalid for everyone, at once.

The paper stays put

Each agreement keeps its own chain of signed events, held by its parties. There is no shared ledger to join, and changing apps does not change the agreement.
