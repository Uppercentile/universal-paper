An open language for agreements

An agreement you can compute.

Universal Paper writes agreements between aggregators, suppliers, and members as data with a fingerprint. Every system that holds a copy computes the same answer, and any change shows. Everything on this page is computed live in your browser.

Vision · what could be built on it

The paper underneath the apps

Universal Paper is meant to sit underneath, the way a file format or a network protocol does. Apps for contracting, procurement, pricing, and settlement can compete on everything above it, and agents can act for parties within signed limits. They interoperate because they all compute the same fingerprints below.

Live events

This is an illustration of a possible future. The apps are hypothetical and none of them exist yet. The parties, events, signatures, verdicts, and fingerprints are real: each scenario is generated and evaluated by the Universal Paper engine as you watch.

Apps compete above

A contract system, an ERP, and a procurement tool can each show the same agreement in their own way. None of them owns it, and none has to trust another’s copy.

Agents act in a sub-tier

An agent signs only under a Mandate its principal signed, with limits on what it may agree to. An action outside those limits is invalid for everyone, at once.

The paper stays put

Each agreement keeps its own chain of signed events, held by its parties. There is no shared ledger to join. Changing apps does not change the agreement.
