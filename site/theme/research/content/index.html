Kinds

Four kinds of entry.

{{KINDS}}

A note is the whole argument. The experiments it ran, the files it produced and the questions it left open are entries of their own, each linked back to the note they came from. You can read one without the others.

Topics

Nine subjects, each defined.

{{TOPICS}}
One primary topic

Every entry has one, and at most two more. The list is fixed and changes on purpose, so there are no near-duplicate tags.

Built on

The Terms, rules and engine calls an entry uses are listed by their real names, so a search for A9 or UP:Admin-Fee finds it.

Language impact

Each entry says whether it needs a change to the language. Most should not.

A number for good

An entry keeps its number and its address, even after it is withdrawn.

Ground rules

How research relates to the language.

01Outside the language

Nothing here is a Term, an Event kind or a rule. The specification is the only source for those.

02Honest about cost

Each entry says what it would add to the language. The first note adds nothing.

03Checkable

Every example names its inputs by fingerprint, so you can recompute it with an engine on your own machine.

04Free to end

An entry may grow, change, or be withdrawn. Agreements do not depend on it.

Status

What the labels mean.

{{LABELS}}

Open and Answered are for questions. If an idea is ever proposed for the language, that happens in the specification, under its own review.
