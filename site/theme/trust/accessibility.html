What we aim for

We want every page on universalpaper.org to be usable with a keyboard, a screen reader, a small screen and reduced motion. Our target is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA.

The site has not yet had an independent accessibility audit, so we do not claim full conformance. This page is an honest account of where it stands.

What the site does today

  • Keyboard use. Navigation, search and controls are built from standard links, buttons and form fields, so they work from the keyboard, and the focused element has a visible outline.
  • Skip link. Every page starts with a “Skip to content” link.
  • Structure. Each page has one main heading, landmark regions and a declared language, so assistive technology can move through it.
  • Reduced motion. If your device asks for reduced motion, animations are reduced or switched off. The home page also has a button to pause its moving artwork.
  • Decorative artwork is hidden from screen readers, and meaningful images have text alternatives.
  • Reflow. Pages fit a phone-width screen without sideways scrolling, and text can be enlarged with your browser’s zoom.

Where it falls short

  • Small touch targets. Some links in the footer and the announcement bar are smaller than the recommended size on phones.
  • Heading levels. A few pages skip a heading level, which can make an outline harder to follow in a screen reader.
  • The Playground. It is a dense, interactive tool and has had less accessibility testing than the rest of the site.
  • Moving content. One panel on the home page changes by itself every few seconds. The pause button stops it.
  • Color contrast has not been measured on every page and state.

We intend to fix these. If one of them blocks you, tell us and we will send you the information another way.

Other ways to read Universal Paper

The whole specification is available as plain Markdown, linked from each specification page, and as a single text file at llms-full.txt. Both work well with screen readers, text browsers and your own styling.

Tell us about a barrier

If something on the site is hard or impossible for you to use, please tell us:

  • email contact@uppercentile.com with the subject “Universal Paper: accessibility”; or
  • open a GitHub issue.

It helps to include the page address, what you were trying to do, and the browser and assistive technology you use.
