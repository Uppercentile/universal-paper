Permissions

What you can do.

Using the language never requires an account, a fee, a license key or a connection to Uppercentile or anyone else. The neutrality charter forbids the core from ever requiring one.

Use Universal Paper for any agreement, commercial or not, at no cost.
Write your own implementation, in any language, open or closed source.
Build and sell products, services and agents that read, write or verify Universal Paper.
Copy, translate, adapt and redistribute the specification and the code.

In return

What the licenses ask.

  • CC BY 4.0

    Give credit, link to the license, and say if you made changes. You may not suggest that Uppercentile endorses your version.

  • Apache-2.0

    Keep the license and the NOTICE file with copies of the code, and mark files you changed.

  • CC0 1.0

    Nothing at all.

Attribution

How to credit Universal Paper.

A line like this satisfies CC BY 4.0 for the specification or the Term library:

"Universal Paper" specification by Uppercentile, https://universalpaper.org, licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: [describe, or "none"].

For the code, keep the LICENSE and NOTICE files. The full license texts ship with the source in the repository.

Contributions

Contributions are accepted under the same licenses: changes to the specification under CC BY 4.0, changes to code under Apache-2.0, and new example agreements and traces under CC0 1.0. Contributors keep their copyright and license their work to everyone on the same terms.

Stewardship, not ownership

Uppercentile created Universal Paper and is its current steward. The steward maintains the specification for everyone who uses it and does not own the language. Products built on Universal Paper, including any Uppercentile offers, are separate from it and may not claim to be required. The steward has committed to hand stewardship to a neutral body once the language has users across segments. See the neutrality charter.
