# UP:Governing-Law — Governing law

**Family:** General · **Spec:** `UP/0.1-draft` · **Machine definition:** [`UP-Governing-Law.json`](UP-Governing-Law.json)

Names the law that governs the Agreement and the forum for disputes.

## Parameters

| Name | Type | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `law` | `string` | Governing law, e.g. a named jurisdiction. |
| `forum` | `string` | Forum for disputes. |

No parameter has a default value (A6).

## Machine definition

No clause forms. This Term has legal meaning only: it changes no State and produces no Commitments.

## Claims

None.

## Human rendering

```text
This Agreement is governed by the law of {{law}}. Disputes are resolved in {{forum}}.
```
